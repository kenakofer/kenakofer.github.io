**[Open the interactive timeline →](https://kenan.schaefkofer.com/LLMTimeline/prototype/boxes.html)** (best in a desktop browser)

Every frontier large language model since 2018, about 230 of them from a dozen labs, each placed at its first public release, with lines showing which models were fine-tuned or distilled from which. We are always open to feedback on how the reference could be improved.

## FAQ:

 **Q**: Which models/companies are included?

 **A**: Flagship models and their size tiers from the major labs, models that Epoch AI flags as frontier or scores on its capabilities index, open-weight models that other models were built from, and the historically important models from before 2023. Dated snapshots of one model are a single box.

 **Q**: How do I read it?

 **A**: Each lab has its own band, which swells when the lab is busy. A box's left edge sits on the model's first public release. Taller boxes were close to the best capability score of their time, and a crown marks a model that topped everything before it at launch. The icons mark open weights, models that were never publicly available, and retired models. Hover a model to trace its lineage, see its lifespan and read its exact dates; switch to the Capability view to lift every model to its score.

 **Q**: Why are the years different widths?

 **A**: Busy years get more room. 2018 to 2022 saw a couple dozen notable models; 2025 alone saw over sixty.

 **Q**: Where does the data come from?

 **A**: Release dates come from the labs' own announcements, cross-checked against [Epoch AI's](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models) model database; deprecation and retirement dates come from the labs' deprecation pages; capability scores are Epoch AI's [Capabilities Index](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/eci). A lineage line is drawn only when the lab or model card documents it, dashed when we haven't confirmed it first-hand. Every date and line has a source in the [dataset on GitHub](https://github.com/kenakofer/LLMTimeline).

 **Q**: I spotted a mistake. Where do I report it?

 **A**: Please [open an issue](https://github.com/kenakofer/LLMTimeline/issues) with a source.
